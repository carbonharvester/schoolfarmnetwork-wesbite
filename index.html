Unused school land can feed a generation.

School Farm Network turns underused land at African schools into professionally managed farms that produce food, create jobs and give young people a pathway into agriculture.

Explore the model Partner with us
The opportunity

The land exists. The operating system does not.

Many African schools hold land that is unused or underproductive. Their students face hunger or unreliable access to meals. Women and young people in the surrounding community need viable work and skills pathways.

Agricultural programmes usually fail for the same reason: no consistent operations, no committed buyers, no financial controls, no evidence. These are not four separate charitable causes. They are one connected systems problem, and it needs one operating system.

How it works

Five stages, one accountable model.

01
Assess
Verify land, water, governance, access, security and commercial viability before anything is built.
02
Build
Establish appropriate irrigation, storage, fencing, soil preparation and operating infrastructure.
03
Grow
Plan and manage crop cycles against real buyer demand and agronomic requirements.
04
Deliver value
Sell or exchange produce and deliver the agreed benefit to the school, including meals.
05
Measure and replicate
Record costs, jobs, yields, school benefits and lessons so the model improves and repeats.

Every outcome comes from the same productive asset, not four disconnected programmes.

Meals
Productive land helps fund or supply meals for students.
Jobs
Farms create paid local work, with emphasis on opportunities for women.
Learning
Schools gain a practical agricultural learning environment.
Future farmers
Young people aged 18 to 35 gain real operational experience and a pathway into agriculture.
Evidence and current stage

Partnership pathways

Different partners, different roles.

Governments and education authorities
Identify suitable school land, set operating standards, create local employment and build an evidence base for wider adoption.
Discuss a government partnership
Foundations and catalytic funders
Catalytic funding establishes infrastructure, tests repeatability, verifies outcomes independently and de-risks a model that can later attract commercial or government capital.
Explore catalytic funding
Buyers and agricultural partners
Demand-led crop planning, quality standards, reliable offtake and transparent transactions, backed by agronomy and implementation expertise.
Talk about offtake and implementation
Schools and school networks
Schools provide land and receive an agreed benefit, primarily meals or value in kind. Eligibility depends on land rights, governance, water, access, security and commercial viability.
