Seeds2Education, Kenya · Our original pilot
The problem

Hungry students. Empty fields. Same fence.

Growing food at scale takes operators, agronomy, buyers, working capital and records, and no one has brought those to the school gate. Programmes hand over seeds and leave. We stay and run the farm.

How it works

One system,
every farm.

01

We assess

Land, water, tenure, access and commercial viability, checked before anything is built. Farms that shouldn’t exist, don’t.

02

We build

Irrigation, storage and fencing, installed by our team. This is where the capital goes.

03

We farm

A paid local crew plants, tends and harvests to a plan, with roles prioritised for women and for people aged 18 to 35.

04

We account

Every cycle is weighed, costed and recorded. What the farm produced, what it paid, what the school received.

The system is the part nobody else builds: operators, infrastructure, agronomy, offtake, data, training and financing, on the ground and in one set of records.
