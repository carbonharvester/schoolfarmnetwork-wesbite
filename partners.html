Partners

The model works because each partner does what they do best.

SFN is designed to work with governments and institutional partners, not around them. Each pathway below explains the partner's role and the value of participating.

Governments and education authorities

SFN can help identify suitable school land, establish operating standards, create local employment and build an evidence base for wider adoption. Government partners set the policy frame; SFN provides the operating capability and the records to evaluate it.

Discuss a government partnership

Foundations and catalytic funders

Catalytic funding establishes farm infrastructure, tests whether the model repeats, funds independent verification of outcomes, and de-risks a model that may later attract commercial or government capital. Funders get evidence, not just activity reports.

Explore catalytic funding

Buyers and food-system partners

Crop planning is demand-led: cycles are planted against real buyer requirements, quality standards and reliable offtake, with transparent transactions recorded on the operating platform. Buyers get planned supply; farms get committed demand.

Talk about offtake

Agricultural and implementation partners

Agronomy, irrigation, soil and water testing, training, insurance, logistics, verification and local delivery. SFN contracts specialist capability rather than rebuilding it, and implementation partners work to shared operating standards.

Offer technical capability

Schools and school networks

Schools provide land and receive an agreed benefit, primarily meals or value in kind. Not every school with land will qualify: eligibility depends on land rights, governance, water, access, security, leadership and commercial viability. Assessment comes before commitment.
