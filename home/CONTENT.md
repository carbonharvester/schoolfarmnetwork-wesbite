# Shared content model for the five finished homepages

One source of truth so the five pages cannot drift apart on facts. Every page below
uses exactly these claims, in exactly these tenses.

## Verified, may be stated as fact

- **Seeds2Education, Kenya** is SFN's original pilot project.
- **Approximately 4 tonnes of onions, harvested and weighed, May 2026.**
  Source: pilot harvest and weighing records. One site, one cycle. A pilot result,
  not portfolio performance.
- A **two-school, two-cycle pilot** is underway.
- Paid roles are **prioritised for women and for young people aged 18 to 35**.

## In progress, future tense only, never written as done

- The harvest **is being sold to market**. Proceeds **will be paid** to Food for
  Education in exchange for student meals.
  **Never write that the harvest was exchanged through Food for Education.** The
  supplier registration ran past the point where schools closed, so no exchange
  happened. This is the single most important copy rule on the site.

## In collection, not yet reportable

Cost per acre, yield per cycle, paid work created, school benefit delivered, and
operational lessons, across the two-school pilot.

## Not yet measured, and said so

Cost per meal. Student learning outcomes.

## Not achieved, stated as ambition

A model repeatable across thousands of schools and tens of thousands of acres.

## Marked `[VERIFY]` on every page

1. Hero wording, per page
2. Named school for the pilot site
3. Plot reference format and plot dimensions
4. Photograph: location, date, credit, and consent for people pictured
5. Measured area of unused or underused land at a named school, with date
6. The eight stage names against the real method, and whether stages repeat in a season
7. Who carries out the assessment, and who signs it off
8. Whether learners take part per stage, and in which subject
9. The real record fields captured at each stage
10. Which crop, variety, planting date and growing period for the crop story
11. Whether produce reaches a school meal, a sale, or both
12. Curriculum links for what the farm teaches
13. Counties, number of schools, and acreage, before any map is published
14. Named people, roles, and consent, before any portrait is published
15. The two calls to action and what happens after each

## Copy rules

- Never headline the tonnage. The proof headline is "First harvest"; the figure
  lives in supporting copy with its source.
- "Feed every generation", never "a generation".
- No em dashes.
- Plain, specific English. Observed nouns, active verbs: measure, mark, plant,
  tend, weigh, record, teach, eat, sell, repeat.
- Empowering, never victim framing.
- Banned: empower, transforming lives, sustainable future, innovation at scale,
  game changing, unlock potential, making an impact.
- No invented people, quotations, schools, case studies, or testimonials.

## The eight stages

01 Assess · 02 Design · 03 Prepare · 04 Plant · 05 Tend · 06 Harvest · 07 Record · 08 Improve

Each answers: who acts, what happens on the land, what is learned, what is
recorded, what is the next decision.

## Four audiences, section "Choose an action"

1. I represent a school.
2. I can deliver or share knowledge.
3. I can fund or resource the work.
4. I want to follow the network.
