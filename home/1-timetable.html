04 · 11:00 · How one farm works

Eight stages, one accountable record.

Every stage answers the same five questions, for one plot, at one school. Open any stage to see the record it keeps.

06 · 13:45 · What the farm teaches

The lesson is the work, not a poster about the work.

Each row below ties a real action on the plot to what a student does with it. Curriculum links are drafted and need checking against the syllabus.

Measure the plot Area, spacing and quantity, worked out on the ground rather than on a worksheet. Verify: which subject and year group
Test the soil and water What the soil is short of, what the crop needs, and what the water will carry. Verify: which subject and year group
Plan a cycle Working backwards from a harvest date to a planting date. Verify: which subject and year group
Keep the books What the cycle cost, what it earned, and whether it was worth running. Verify: which subject and year group
Do the paid work Real operational experience, with roles prioritised for women and for people aged 18 to 35. A route into agriculture as a job, not a lesson about one.
