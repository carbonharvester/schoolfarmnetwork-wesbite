02 · The line that stayed flat

Ruled, and then left alone.

A school day has periods with nothing scheduled on them. A school site has ground with nothing planted on it. Both were ruled and then left, and a line on its own feeds nobody.

Verify: measured area of unused or underused land at a named school, with the date it was measured

03 · The turn

The same stroke, planted.

Measure the ground that is free, check the water and mark the boundary. The line stops being a rule on a page and becomes a row in the soil.

06 · What it teaches

The lesson is the work.

Measure the plot Area, spacing and quantity, worked out on the ground rather than on a worksheet. Verify: subject and year
Test soil and water What the soil is short of, what the crop needs, and what the water will carry. Verify: subject and year
Plan a cycle Working backwards from a harvest date to a planting date. Verify: subject and year
Keep the books What the cycle cost, what it earned, and whether it was worth running. Verify: subject and year
Do the paid work Real operational experience, with roles prioritised for women and for people aged 18 to 35. A route into agriculture as a job, not a lesson about one.
