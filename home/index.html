School Farm Network · finished homepages

Five finished homepages. Pick one to take forward.

Each is a complete homepage, not a pilot: navigation, hero, the missed opportunity, the signature transformation, all eight stages, the crop story, what the farm teaches, the evidence ledger, the network, people, choose an action, and a footer. They share the same facts and the same tenses, held in CONTENT.md so they cannot drift apart. What differs is the idea, the design system, and the way the page is actually navigated.

01

A Living Timetable

Architecture · vertical scroll with a sticky timetable rail

Device: ruled timetable rows carrying clock times. On scroll the rows lengthen into crop beds and the times are replaced in place by growing stages. The rail is a school day, so chapter navigation is diegetic. The eight stages open as accordions.

Show Your Working

Architecture · a paced sequence, one point per screen

Device: the squared exercise book. The farm is set out as a worked problem and anything unmeasured is an empty box, never a plausible number. Ten questions advanced on purpose by button, arrow key or swipe, with a read-as-one-page mode for anyone who wants to scan.

Site Visit

Architecture · spatial and non-linear

Device: the school ground is the page. Ten survey pegs sit on a plan, from the classrooms to the kitchen to the gate, and every part of the site is attached to one. The visitor sets the order. Choosing the measurement peg resolves the hatched unknown ground into a plot with beds. A text view exists for reading and for small screens.

One Line

Architecture · a horizontal filmstrip

Device: a single unbroken stroke. The page reads sideways and the line runs along a constant baseline through all ten panels, thickening into a planting row where the ground is turned. Native horizontal scroll with snap, arrow keys and dots; it stacks vertically on small screens.
