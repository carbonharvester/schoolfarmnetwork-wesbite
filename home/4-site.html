Peg 02 · The unused ground

Nobody has put a tape across it.

A school day has periods with nothing scheduled on them. A school site has ground with nothing planted on it. The school already has both.

Until the ground is measured there is nothing to plan, nothing to cost, and nothing to argue for.

Verify: measured area of unused or underused land at a named school, with the date it was measured

Peg 03 · The measurement

Measuring is what turns ground into a plot.

Walk the site. Measure the ground that is free. Check the water source and mark the boundary. The hatched area on the plan becomes a defined plot with beds laid out in it.

The dimensions stay marked unknown, because they have not been supplied.

Verify: plot dimensions, water source, and boundary

Peg 06 · What it teaches

The lesson is the work, not a poster about the work.

Measure the plot
Area, spacing and quantity, worked out on the ground. Verify: subject
Test soil and water
What the soil is short of and what the water will carry. Verify: subject
Plan a cycle
Working backwards from a harvest date to a planting date. Verify: subject
Keep the books
What the cycle cost, what it earned, whether it was worth running. Verify: subject
Do the paid work
Real operational experience, roles prioritised for women and for people aged 18 to 35.
