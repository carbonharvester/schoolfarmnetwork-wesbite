Land, water, governance, access, security, commercial viability. Every school passes all six before a single row is planted.

Unused school land can feed every generation.

School Farm Network turns underused land at African schools into professionally managed farms that produce food, create jobs and give young people a pathway into agriculture. This page is the register. It lists what exists, and what does not yet.

Evidence and current stage

Verified · May 2026
First harvest. Approximately 4 tonnes of onions, harvested and weighed at Seeds2Education, the original pilot project. Source: pilot harvest and weighing records, May 2026. A pilot result, not portfolio performance.
In progress
The harvest is being sold to market. Proceeds will be paid to Food for Education in exchange for student meals.
In collection
