Five directions for School Farm Network.

Each one traces to a specific site found by searching the refero gallery against SFN's actual business, not picked by taste. Real tokens were pulled off the running pages. No direction reuses a typeface from an earlier attempt.

The research trail, including what was searched and why each reference was kept, is in RESEARCH.md. Copy and evidence are identical across all five, so you are comparing design and nothing else.

01

The Register

After Piet Oudolf · oudolf.com

The homepage is the network itself. Every school named in large type over a wall of the real pilot photography, veiled to a flat warm grey. Hover a school and its own land brightens behind the name. Sites not yet built are drawn as outlined type, so the page shows the frame being filled.

Why it could work: evidence-led by construction, and it grows as the network grows. Risk: with one site live it is a short list, and that honesty has to read as confidence rather than emptiness.

02

Almanac

After Ambrook · ambrook.com

The closest living business analogue: financial software for working farms. Its real tokens, bone canvas, warm near-black ink and wheat accent, with a forest-green ledger panel. The custom layer is hand-drawn engravings in the manner of an agricultural almanac, and the evidence is presented as an accounting table.

Why it could work: reads as an operating company, not a cause. Risk: the calmest of the five, so it carries the least shock.

03

Data Portrait

After W.E.B. Du Bois, Paris 1900 · via Vocal Type

Found by following Break Maiden's typeface back to its foundry. In 1900 Du Bois argued for Black American life in front of a sceptical world using hand-drawn geometric charts in cream, crimson, ochre and green. The page becomes a set of numbered plates: the six assessment conditions as a fan, the evidence record as bars where the unmeasured column is drawn as an empty dashed box.

Why it could work: the argument is built into the form, and no NGO site looks remotely like it. Risk: the strongest point of view, so the most polarising.

04

Broadside

After Break Maiden · breakmaiden.co

Colossal condensed capitals set at a line height tight enough that the lines crowd each other, with arrow glyphs inline in the sentence, over hard blocks of maize, clay, leaf and kraft. This is the boldness you liked, arrived at from the gallery rather than from the one name already in hand, and in earth colour rather than neon.

Why it could work: loudest and most confident in a room of NGO decks. Risk: volume can outrun the evidence when the evidence is one harvest.

05

Aerial

After INVERSA, Munro Partners and Ecosia

Land from above as the canvas, the drone film running full bleed, a terse two-line declarative at the bottom left and the wordmark set colossal down the right edge. Beneath it a cream band of plain-spoken text and counters that only count the figures that are real, with cost per meal left as a dash because it has not been measured.

Why it could work: the investor register, land first, claims second. Risk: closest to the territory already rejected as a photography site.
