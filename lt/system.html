Type

  • Fraunces, display only, optical sizing set explicitly (opsz 144 for h1 and h2, 48 for record headings, 20 for the wordmark), SOFT 0 WONK 0. Weight 600.
  • IBM Plex Sans for interface and body. 400 and 500. Body 17px, 1.6.
  • IBM Plex Mono for times, plot references, field labels, measurements and verification marks. 11 to 13px, tracking 0.08 to 0.13em, uppercase.
  • Scale: 68 / 52 / 32 / 27 / 20 / 17 / 15 / 12 / 11.
  • Licences: all three are SIL Open Font License 1.1 and can be shipped. Self host before production.

Structure and motion

  • Grid: 12 columns desktop with a 180px fixed timetable rail; 8 at tablet; 4 at mobile where the rail becomes a 3px progress marker.
  • Row unit: 76px desktop, 70px tablet, 60px mobile. Every hero element is placed on this row, including the marked plot.
  • Container 1400px, padding 40 / 28 / 20.
  • Radius 2px. Line weights: hairline 1px rules, 1.5px section openers, 2px on the marked plot.
  • Motion: 180ms linear for scrub-driven values, 420ms cubic-bezier(.22,.7,.24,1) for state changes. Two signature behaviours only, of which the pilot contains the first.
  • Accessibility: focus ring 2px ink blue at 3px offset, inverting to crop-dark on the blackboard; semantic h1 to h3; decorative rules aria-hidden; the transition resolves to its end state under prefers-reduced-motion and with no JavaScript.

Deliverable 5

Factual verification list

Every claim or label in the pilot that needs your input. Each one is marked in the interface with an orange rule, so nothing plausible is passing itself off as confirmed.

  1. Hero support sentence. Is "helps schools turn available land into places to learn, grow food, build practical skills, and create wider opportunity" the approved description?
  2. Primary and secondary actions. The brief proposes "Start a school farm" and "Back the network"; the pilot uses "See how a school farm starts". Confirm which is real, and what happens after the click.
  3. Plot reference format. Is "Plot 01" how sites are actually identified?
  4. Plot dimensions, and the units used.
  5. IMG-01: location, date, photographer credit, and informed consent for the people pictured.
  6. Measured area of unused or underused land at a named school, with the date it was measured. This is the one fact the missed opportunity screen needs.
  7. The eight stage names, and whether stages repeat within a single season.
  8. Who carries out the assessment, and who signs it off.
  9. Whether learners take part at the assessment stage, and in which subject.
  10. The real record fields captured at assessment.
  11. Who takes the go or wait decision at the end of stage 01.
  12. Countries, number of schools, and acreage, for section 8 and the network view.
  13. Whether the school retains the produce, sells it, or both. Needed for section 5.

Resolved without input: font licensing. Fraunces, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono are all SIL OFL 1.1 and can be shipped.

Design gauntlet

Three rounds, rendered at 1440 × 1000 and 390 × 844

Scored against visible evidence only. Any category below 4 is a failure and was revised before rescoring.

Round 1 — three failures

  • Accessibility 2. The header action rendered as a solid black rectangle with no visible label. .nav-links a beat .btn on specificity and set the text to muted dark on the dark fill.
  • Editorial hierarchy 2. The hero action, the marked plot and its caption occupied the same row and overlapped.
  • Mobile integrity 1. The sticky rail forced a viewport-height row that left a dead band under the hero, and at 390 the clock times sat underneath the headline and body copy.

Fixed: scoped the colour rule to a:not(.btn); moved the plot to row 6 and its caption to row 7; replaced the in-grid rail with a fixed rail and indented each section past it; moved all mobile content clear of the time gutter.

Round 2 — two failures

  • Concept ownership 3. During the transition the clock time and the stage name were both half visible in the same place, so the labels read as garbled overprints rather than a substitution.
  • Mobile integrity 2. The record's five fields were being placed in the 74px number rail, wrapping to two or three words per line.
  • Also noted, not a failure: five verification marks in one viewport read as a broken page rather than a rigorous one.

Fixed: the time now clears out by 31% of the row's progress and the stage arrives from 55%, with a 6px counter shift, so the two never coexist; the record collapses to a true single column at 390; verification marks reduced to the fields that are genuinely unknown, with a single footnote covering drafted wording.

Proposal

How the system extends, if this is approved

  1. 5. At the table. One crop followed from the plot to its destination, laid out as a continuation of the same record: variety, planting date, growing period, labour, harvest, use, lesson.
  2. 6. What the farm teaches. A stepped lesson plan built on the row unit, each step tied to a real action already shown in the record. No benefits grid.
  3. 7. Evidence ledger. Reuses the record's field structure, split three ways into outputs, outcomes, and learning, including what failed. Numbers are never animated.
  4. 8. Beyond the gate. The second signature motion. A completed record contracts to a single plotted point and joins verified sites on a map. If no verified dataset exists, one prototype point is shown and the network view is described as forthcoming.
  5. 9 to 11. Named people with roles and consent, a guided four-way action chooser built as four timetable rows, and a footer that returns to the timetable with the final row reserved for the next verified school.

Nothing above is built. The pilot stops at section 4 as instructed.
