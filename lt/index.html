School Farm Network · pilot routes

Five routes. One pilot scope. Same truths.

Each route commits to a different proprietary device, and each has its own typography and palette. They are not variations of one theme. Every route covers the same five pilot sections, carries the same copy, and marks the same open facts, so what differs between them is the idea and nothing else.

Pilot sections in every route: navigation, the hero, the missed opportunity, the signature transformation, and the first view of how one farm works. Sections 5 to 11 are not built in any of them. Route A also has a written system, media manifest, verification list and gauntlet.

A

A Living Timetable

Device: ruled timetable rows carrying clock times. As you scroll, the rows lengthen into crop beds and the times are replaced in place by growing stages. The hero marks one plot inside a school day.

Fraunces · IBM Plex Blackboard + paper Motion: timetable to field

Weakest point: the network is only implied, because the zoom-out is section 8 and outside pilot scope.

Open → B

Roll Call

Device: the attendance register. A mark against a name means a school is planted. One row is marked and the rest are openly blank. On the turn, the mark lifts off the ruled page and lands as a located point, with every unverified school drawn hollow.

Literata · Karla · Courier Prime Register paper + red ruling Motion: mark to map

Strongest on: showing a network rather than one garden. Risk: an almost empty register has to read as ambition, not as thinness.

Open → C

Show Your Working

Device: the squared exercise book. The farm is set out as a worked problem, and every quantity that has not been measured is an empty box rather than a plausible number. The answer box reads "not yet measured". On the turn, the working writes itself line by line in the order the work happens.

Instrument Serif · Instrument Sans · Space Mono Graph paper + marking pen Motion: the working writes itself

Strongest on: credibility. Honesty is structural, so it cannot be quietly dropped later. Risk: a page of empty boxes is a hard first impression for a funder.

Open → D

One Line

Device: a single unbroken line. It begins as a ruled line on a school page, thickens into a planting row, carries a harvest, and ends as the rim of a plate. School and farm are literally the same stroke. The line then becomes the spine the record hangs from.

Syne · Manrope Bone + ink + one orange Motion: the line travels and changes

Strongest on: boldness, and uniting school and farm in one idea. Risk: it promises a plate at the end, which is the least verified claim we have.

Open → E

Measured

Device: the survey drawing. Every element carries a dimension, and anything not yet measured is dimensioned as unknown. The navigation is a drawing title block. On the turn, the scale bar zooms out from one metre to the plot, the school grounds, and the network, recalibrating as it goes.

Chivo · DM Sans · DM Mono Drafting sheet + survey cyan Motion: scale zoom to network

Strongest on: making the evidence discipline the visual language rather than a section. Risk: the most technical, and the furthest from warmth.
