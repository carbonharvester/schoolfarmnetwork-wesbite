02 · The blank register

A school can hold land for years without anyone marking it.

A school day has periods with nothing scheduled on them. A school site has ground with nothing planted on it. Nobody is keeping a record of either, so nothing changes.

Verify: measured area of unused or underused land at a named school, with the date it was measured
