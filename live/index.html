The land is already there.The system isn’t.

School Farm Network turns underused land at African schools into professionally operated farms that produce food, create jobs, train the next generation of farmers and help fund school meals.

Seeds2Education
Kenya · Our original pilot

We assess. We build. We farm. We account.

Growing food at scale takes operators, agronomy, buyers, working capital and records, and no one has brought those to the school gate. Programmes hand over seeds and leave. We stay and run the farm, with a paid local crew and roles prioritised for women and for people aged 18 to 35.
